# Game Modes

**English** · [Deutsch](Spielmodi)

All modes start from the menu under **Spielen** (Play). The browser remembers your tribe and game settings (map size, map type, AI difficulty, number of opponents, starting stars).

| Mode | Players | Server needed | Saved |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gegen KI** (vs. AI) | you against 1–5 AI tribes | no | in the browser, **Weiterspielen** (Continue) in the menu |
| **Hotseat** | 2–5 people on one device | no | in the browser |
| **Online** | 2–5 people, optionally 0–3 AI | yes | on the server, reachable via the link |
| **Tutorial** | you against an easy AI, with guidance | no | in the browser |
| **Daily challenge** | the same map for everyone, 30 turns | only for the leaderboard | in the browser |
| **Weekly challenge** | fixed map with a special rule | only for the leaderboard | in the browser |
| **Kampagne** (Campaign) | 13 missions | no | in the browser |

## Hotseat

A handover screen appears between turns so that nobody sees the other player's map.

## Online

1. Choose **Online** – the game creates a match and shows a link.
2. Share the link. Whoever opens it takes a free seat.
3. Whoever's turn it is plays; the others see the moves as soon as they are saved.

Once all seats are taken, only the players can see the match. Spectating is only possible after the end, otherwise the fog could be bypassed with a second window.

AI opponents in online matches are computed by the device of the player who just ended their turn. If that player closes the tab before the AI is done, another player's device takes over after two minutes.

**Your online matches** are listed in the menu under **Spielen** (Play), with the ones where it is your turn first. With the profile code you can see them on another device too.

**Resigning and absent players:** in the menu of a running match, **Aufgeben** (Resign) hands your tribe over to the AI, and the match continues for the others. If a player stops showing up, you can fill a seat that was never taken with the AI right away, and hand the tribe of a player who has not moved for 24 hours over to the AI. Matches that are idle for 30 days are deleted.

**When it's your turn:** when your opponent's move arrives, you hear a sound. If the tab is in the background, its title flashes “Du bist am Zug” (It's your turn). In the game settings, under “Mitteilung, wenn du online am Zug bist” (Notify me when it's my turn online), you can also turn on browser notifications. On iPad and iPhone, Safari only shows notifications for installed web apps; there the sound and title remain.
