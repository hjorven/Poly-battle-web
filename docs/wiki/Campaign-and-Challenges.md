## Weekly challenge

Every week brings a fixed map with one of these special rules:

- **Clear Skies**: No fog – everyone sees the whole map.
- **Fortresses**: Every city starts with a city wall.
- **Gold Rush**: All tribes start with 25 stars.
- **Seafarer**: Island map, everyone knows Fishing and Sailing.
- **Titans**: Every capital starts with a giant.
- **Blitz**: Only 20 turns – score points fast.

## Daily challenge

Every day, everyone gets the same map and the same tribe for 30 turns. Your score counts. With a name you appear on the leaderboard.
