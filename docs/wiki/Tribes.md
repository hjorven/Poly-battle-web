## AI personalities

Every AI tribe gets a personality at the start of the game. It decides how eager the tribe is to attack, build or make peace.

- **Conqueror** – for example: “Your walls look fragile.”
- **Merchant** – for example: “Welcome, stranger! Trade helps us both.”
- **Seafarer** – for example: “Ahoy! The sea belongs to everyone – mostly to me.”
- **Builder** – for example: “Behold our towers! Keep your distance.”
