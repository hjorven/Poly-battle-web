# How to Play

**English** · [Deutsch](Spielanleitung)

The game is in German. Button names are shown in German as in the game, with an English translation in brackets.

## The goal

In **Eroberung** (Domination) mode, the last tribe that still owns cities wins. In **Perfektion** (Perfection) mode, the game ends after 30 turns and the highest score wins. You earn points for cities, population, technologies, units, explored land, buildings, ruins, lighthouses and monuments.

## A turn

1. **Collect stars.** At the start of your turn your cities produce stars – the more population, buildings and resources, the more stars.
2. **Move and attack with units.** Each unit may move and attack once per turn (some abilities allow a different order).
3. **Spend.** Tap a city and use the toolbar to train units, buy population or construct buildings via **Ausbauen** (Build); unlock technologies under **Forschung** (Research).
4. **End your turn** with **Zug beenden** (End turn). Then the other tribes take their turns.

There is no undo – every move counts.

## Moving and attacking safely

Moving and attacking take two taps: the first marks the target in yellow, the second carries it out. That way nothing happens by accident. When attacking, a **battle preview** appears at the target after the first tap: both units with their health before and after, the damage and whether there will be a counterattack.

Tapping one of your own cities selects it. To move a unit into the city, double-tap the city quickly.

## The interface

The map comes first; everything else sits in floating cards along the edges:

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| top left | your stars (large), below them how many you will get next turn; the turn number |
| top centre | all tribes with their stars; the tribe whose turn it is has a green outline |
| top right | research, overview, sound, menu, **bereit** (ready – jumps to the next unit that can still act) and **Zug beenden** (End turn) |
| bottom right | minimap (tap to jump there), with the camera buttons above it |

**City or unit selected:** a sign appears right at the object – for cities with their level (five hexagons), income and rewards, for units with a health bar and stats (health, attack, defence, movement, range). The **toolbar** sits at the bottom centre: for cities, Build, Population +1 and every unit you can train (cost in the hexagon); for units, the possible actions such as building a road, upgrading or founding a city, plus **Details**. If not everything fits, swipe the toolbar sideways or scroll with the mouse wheel. Locked items tell you why.

**Unit details:** **Details** in the toolbar (or key I, or tapping the selected unit again) expands the toolbar: abilities (tap to get an explanation), terrain and defence factor, healing, progress towards veteran status and **Auflösen** (Disband).

**Overview:** tabs for cities (level, income and what needs doing: choose a reward, train, under threat), army, diplomacy and monuments with progress (“12 of 20 technologies”). Tapping a city or unit jumps to it.

**Light or dark:** in the menu under **Spielen** (Play), game settings, **Darstellung** (Appearance): system, light or dark.

## Developing cities

**Ausbauen** (Build) in the toolbar opens a 3D view of the city area: drag to rotate, mouse wheel or two fingers to zoom. Each tile shows what is there and what it yields. Tiles marked orange can be built on right away. Tap a tile and choose the building on the right; each one shows how many extra stars per turn it brings – including when a neighbour benefits too (for example a windmill next to a new farm, marked green). Tap the city tile itself to train units there as well. Things that need more research are listed in small print below.

When a city grows to a new level, you choose a reward (see [Cities and Buildings](Cities-and-Buildings)).

## Mercenary camps

Every map has a few tent camps away from the starting cities. Each one offers a specific unit, for example a knight or a crossbowman. Move one of your land units onto the camp and choose **Anwerben** (Hire): the unit appears next to it, even if you have not researched the matching technology yet. Every tribe can use each camp once; the price is shown on the button (see [Cities and Buildings](Cities-and-Buildings)).

## Fog of war

Undiscovered land lies under fog. Units and cities reveal their surroundings; mountains and hills give more sight. Land that has been discovered but is not currently observed is darkened – you see no foreign units there, and cities show the state in which you last saw them. The same applies to the AI: it only plans with what its tribe currently sees or has already discovered, and it has to find you first.

## Grace period and diplomacy

On **Leicht** (Easy) the AI does not attack you until turn 12, on **Normal** until turn 6, on **Schwer** (Hard) there is no grace period – unless you attack first. With the **Diplomacy** technology you can offer peace in the overview.

## Controls

| Action | Mouse and keyboard | Touch (tablet, phone) |
|---|---|---|
| Pan the map | drag or arrow keys | drag with one finger |
| Zoom | mouse wheel or + and − | pinch with two fingers |
| Rotate / tilt | right mouse button, Q/E or R/F | two-finger rotation or the buttons at the bottom right |
| Default view | N | button N |
| Select | click | tap |

| Key | Effect |
|---|---|
| Space | next unit that can still act |
| Enter | end turn |
| T | research |
| O | overview |
| B | last turn report |
| I | info on the selected unit |
| H | go to capital |
| M | sound on/off |
| Esc | clear selection, close window |

In a city's build window, the arrow keys switch tiles and the digits 1–9 build the matching option. In the research view, dragging moves the tree, mouse wheel or two fingers zoom, and a double tap resets the view. If the tree does not fit the window (iPad, phone), the buttons at the top jump straight to one of the five branches.

## Hints from the AI apprentice

The **Tipp** (Hint) button at the top right asks the AI what it would do in your place right now: attack, move, train, build or research. The suggestion is marked on the map with a short explanation. **Ausführen** (Execute) plays it like a normal move, **Schließen** (Close) lets you decide yourself. Daily and weekly challenges played with hints do not count for the leaderboard.

## Replay after the game

When a game is over, **Karte ansehen** (View map) shows the whole world without fog and a bar at the bottom: the slider jumps to any turn, **Abspielen** (Play) runs the game – slow, normal or as a **Zeitraffer** (time-lapse). That way you can see who captured which city and when.

## Turn report

After the other tribes have moved, the turn report on the right edge shows what happened – grouped into “against you”, “in the world” and “at home”. Tapping an entry jumps to that spot on the map. Press **B** or use the overview to bring it back.
